Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Short version: we only use your details to respond to you.

1. What we collect

When you contact us — through the enquiry form, email, phone, or Instagram — we receive the details you share: your name, email address, phone number, business name, and your message. We do not collect sensitive personal information, and we ask you not to send any.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare project quotes.
  • To communicate about ongoing or future work you request.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your details with third parties for marketing.

3. Email delivery

Enquiries sent through our contact form are delivered to socialframecreative@gmail.com using EmailJS, a third-party email delivery service. Your message passes through their infrastructure under their own privacy terms, which you can review at emailjs.com.

4. Data retention

We keep enquiry correspondence only as long as needed for the conversation and any resulting project, then delete it on request.

5. Your rights

You may ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, at any time by emailing socialframecreative@gmail.com or calling +91 9911445939.
