WHO WE ARE

We Believe Social Media Should Feel Intentional

Social Frame Creative is a creative studio in New Delhi crafting scroll-stopping social media design for brands that take their story seriously. Every visual with a purpose. Every campaign communicating clearly. Every brand recognisable.

WHY SOCIAL FRAME EXISTS

Small brands deserved better design

We kept seeing the same gap: large companies with polished creative systems, and growing businesses stuck with mismatched templates and rushed posts. Social Frame was started to close that gap — agency-quality creative direction, scoped honestly for startups, local businesses, and growing brands.

We don’t claim decades of history, awards, or a roster of famous clients. What we offer instead is something verifiable in every delivery: disciplined process, clear communication, and design that respects your customer’s intelligence.

How we think about brands

A brand is not a logo — it’s the pattern customers learn to recognise. Colors, type, tone, rhythm: repeated consistently, they become familiarity; familiarity becomes trust; trust becomes business. Our entire practice is built around creating and protecting that pattern for you.

OUR CREATIVE PHILOSOPHY

Three beliefs behind everything we make

1. Every visual should have a purpose

Decoration is the enemy of communication. Before designing, we define the single job each creative must do — then judge the result against that job, not against trends.

2. Every campaign should communicate clearly

If a viewer needs more than a second to understand the message, the design failed. Hierarchy, contrast, and restraint are our primary tools — not effects.

3. Every brand should feel recognisable

Consistency compounds. We build reusable systems so your hundredth post strengthens the same identity your first post introduced.

WHAT WE VALUE

Working principles, not wall posters

01

Clarity over cleverness

A design the client’s customer understands beats a design designers admire. We choose the readable option every time.

02

Systems over one-offs

Single beautiful posts fade. Documented systems — type, color, layouts — keep delivering value long after handover.

03

Honesty over hype

No invented metrics, no guaranteed virality, no fake urgency. Clear scopes, realistic timelines, and straight answers.

04

Craft in the details

Alignment, spacing, export quality, file naming — the invisible work is what makes the visible work feel premium.

05

Partnership over transactions

We ask about your business, not just your brief — because better context produces better creative, month after month.

06

Respect for budgets

Most of our clients are growing businesses. We scope tightly, reuse intelligently, and never sell work you don’t need.

WHO WE WORK WITH

Built for growing businesses

Startups finding their voice, local businesses competing with chains, creators turning expertise into authority, and established brands refreshing tired visuals. If you care how your brand looks and what it says — we’ll fit.

OUR VISION

A more creative and connected world

We want every brand — regardless of size — to have access to design that communicates with intention. A world where small businesses look as considered as the biggest names, and customers can trust what they see.
